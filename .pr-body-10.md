## Summary

First, verifiable slice of Phase 7 (Remote Control, Feature 15). Remote and mobile control starts with explicit device pairing and revocable grants.

- src/device-pairing.ts: a PairedDevice (identity, authorization scope, creation/last-seen times, revoke flag) and a DeviceRegistry. registerDevice (no clobber, stores a copy), revokeDevice, touchDevice (last-seen), isAuthorized (present, unrevoked, in scope), listDevices. Authorization scope is the small set of remote actions the ROADMAP lists (view tasks/state, receive attention, approve/deny, answer questions, stop/resume, view diffs).
- src/device-pairing.test.ts: 6 tests.

## Why
The ROADMAP requires explicit device pairing with revocable grants before any remote control surface exists. This lands the trust boundary as pure, tested state that the transport and approval UI build on.
