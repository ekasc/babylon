## Summary

First, verifiable slice of Phase 8 (Automation, Feature 16: Scheduled and Conditional Tasks). After background execution is reliable, Babylon tasks can run without an open foreground session.

- src/automation.ts: trigger model (interval, daily, file_watch, branch_watch), a ScheduledTask/ScheduledTaskRegistry, register (no clobber), enable/disable, remove, recordRun, and a pure evaluateTrigger(now, lastRunAt, event) plus listDueTasks. Invalid trigger configs (non-positive interval, out-of-range daily time) never fire, and daily fires once per day at the scheduled time.
- src/automation.test.ts: 10 tests.

## Why
The ROADMAP lets Babylon run tasks on a schedule or on a file/branch change once background execution is reliable. This lands the trigger/evaluation core as pure, tested state that the scheduler and executor build on.
